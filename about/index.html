I am a researcher and student at École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne (EPFL) in Switzerland. My research focuses on spectral graph theory, dynamical systems on complex networks, and geometric deep learning.

This blog serves as an open academic repository of mathematical expositions, research notes, and interactive visualizations for studying networks, PDEs on graphs, and spectral graph algorithms.

Contact via Email GitHub Profile

Research Interests

  • Spectral Graph Theory: Eigenvalue distributions of graph Laplacians, algebraic connectivity, nodal domains, and Cheeger's inequality.
  • Continuous & Discrete Dynamical Systems: Heat equation and wave equation on graphs, consensus dynamics, Kuramoto synchronization, and random walks.
  • Graph Neural Networks (GNNs): Spectral convolutions, over-smoothing and over-squashing analysis, curvature-based rewiring (Ricci curvature).
  • Scientific Computing & Numerical Optimization: Sparse matrix solvers, Krylov subspace methods, and scalable graph partitioning algorithms.
