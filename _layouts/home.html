Research Topics

🕸️

Spectral Graph Theory

Graph Laplacians, algebraic connectivity, Fiedler vectors, Cheeger bounds, and continuous spectrum analysis on infinite and random graphs.

⚡

Dynamical Systems on Networks

Heat diffusion, consensus protocols, epidemic spreading (SIS/SIR models), synchronization of Kuramoto oscillators, and random walks.

🧠

Geometric Deep Learning

Message passing graph neural networks (MPNNs), spectral convolutions, over-smoothing phenomena, and Ricci curvature on graphs.
